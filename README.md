## 🌟 Sections & Features

- **Navbar:** Displays the original uploaded **Nexa Aquatics logo** (`/assets/nexa-logo.png`) on the left side, clearly visible on both desktop and mobile without cropping or distortion. Smooth navigation to all sections, quick Call and WhatsApp buttons.
- **Hero:** Cinematic staggered text reveal, live statistics, and the interactive **Jumping Fish Simulator** (Golden Arowana jumping out of water with splash ripples and Web Audio procedural splash sound effect).
- **Retail Section (`#retail`):** Dedicated showcase for individual hobbyists and home aquariums with product cards, specifications, and pre-filled WhatsApp enquiry buttons.
- **Wholesale Section (`#wholesale`):** Dedicated bulk ordering section for pet shops and commercial reselling, detailing EPS thermocol master crates, double oxygen-packed bags, 100% Live Arrival Guarantee, and minimum order quantities (MOQs).
- **Fish Breeds (`#breeds`):** Comprehensive exotic ornamental fish catalog (Super Red Arowana, Show Bettas, Discus, Flowerhorn, Koi, etc.) with detailed water parameters (pH, temperature, size).
- **About Sanctuary (`#about`):** Dedication to ethical fish husbandry and the 21-day medical quarantine protocol.
- **Services (`#services`):** Turnkey luxury nature aquascaping, marine reef setups, and monthly tank concierge.
- **Gallery (`#gallery`):** High-resolution installation portfolio with full-screen lightbox modal.
- **Location Section (`#location`):** Google Maps embed centered on `HQQJ+75, Thandarai, Tamil Nadu 635110`, Get Directions button, visiting hours, and contact details.
- **Contact Section (`#contact`):** Interactive inquiry form supporting both Retail and Wholesale orders, sending pre-formatted messages directly to WhatsApp.
- **Floating WhatsApp:** Persistent floating quick-chat widget with fast options for retail and wholesale inquiries.

## 🎨 Animations

1. **Fish Swimming Continuously in Background:** Natural sinusoidal swimming trajectories with directional flips.
2. **Floating Water Bubbles:** Dynamic continuous bubbles rising upwards with horizontal sway.
3. **Fish Jumping Out of Water & Returning:** Parabolic jump arc above waterline with splash droplets and concentric ripples.
4. **Smooth Scrolling Animations:** GSAP ScrollTrigger reveals and native smooth scrolling.
5. **Text Reveal Animations:** Staggered entrance typography in Hero and About sections.
6. **Image Hover Animations:** 3D card tilt and scale zoom effects.
7. **Smooth Page Transitions & Modals:** Lightbox dialogs and mobile navigation drawer.
